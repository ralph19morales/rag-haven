# Administrative issuances and court rules that decide what a patient is
# actually charged, and how an unpaid bill is collected.

# Fetch with:
#   python fetch/fetch_seeds.py --file fetch/seeds_billing_issuances.txt --subdir billing

# Statutes state the entitlement; these issuances carry the operative detail a
# billing question turns on — which patients are No-Balance-Billing eligible,
# how the discount is computed on a hospital bill, what medical assistance a
# government hospital can absorb, and the procedure for suing on the balance.

# --- Collecting the balance: the procedure an unpaid bill goes through ---
# Small claims (no lawyers, money claims up to P1,000,000) is the ordinary
# route for an unpaid hospital or professional fee; this consolidated 2022
# issuance superseded the 2016 small-claims rules.
# CURATED after fetching: the PDF's second half is a comparative table setting
# the 1991, 2016 and 2022 rules side by side — the same rule text three times
# over. Indexed whole, those near-duplicate chunks crowd the real provision out
# of top-k. The saved .txt keeps the rules proper and the forms and drops the
# tables; the .pdf was removed. Re-fetching skips it while that .txt exists.
2022 Rules on Expedited Procedures in the First Level Courts (AM No. 08-8-7-SC) - Small Claims and Summary Procedure | https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/08-8-7-SC-1.pdf

# --- Deceased patient: release and disposal of the remains ---
