# Philippine Supreme Court jurisprudence on medical malpractice / negligence
# and the doctrine of informed consent.
#
# Format:  <name> | <url>   (full-text HTML from the Supreme Court E-Library)
# Fetch with:  python fetch/fetch_seeds.py --file fetch/seeds_jurisprudence.txt --subdir jurisprudence
# URLs verified live 2026-07.
#
# Why these matter: doctrines like "informed consent" and the standard of care
# for medical negligence are defined by the Supreme Court, NOT by statute — so
# a corpus of only statutes/PRC/DOH rules cannot answer questions about them.

# --- The landmark informed-consent case ---
Dr. Rubi Li v. Spouses Soliman (G.R. 165279) - Doctrine of Informed Consent | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/37801

# --- More informed-consent jurisprudence (added 2026-09-13) ---
# Most recent SC application of the Li v. Soliman four-element test: signed
# consent forms plus testimony that risks were explained defeated the claim —
# "less risk" was held not to mean "risk-free". Verified full text via
# elibrary; the SC's own PDF of this decision is a scanned/no-text-layer copy
# behind a CloudFront 403 that needs a browser UA, so the elibrary HTML below
# is the better source (real text, no OCR needed).
Que v. Philippine Heart Center (G.R. 268308) - Informed Consent, Stenting Procedure, Four-Element Test Applied | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/23/69891

# Distinct doctrinal angle none of the above cover: consent BY A GUARDIAN for a
# patient who cannot consent for himself (an adult ward with a mental age of
# about 8), for an irreversible-adjacent procedure (bilateral vasectomy). The
# EN BANC resolution engages RA 10354's "free, informed consent" framework at
# length (68 mentions of "consent", 92 of "guardian" in the full text) rather
# than disposing of it purely on the procedural point (the petitioner-nun died
# during the appeal). Verified full text via elibrary before adding — an
# unrelated search result once mischaracterized a different case (Ruñez v.
# Jurado, an SC internal personnel matter with zero mentions of "refuse" and
# one of "consent") as being on point; always check the actual text, not the
# search summary.
Sister Pilar Versoza v. People (G.R. 184535) - Guardian Consent for Sterilization of a Ward Lacking Capacity | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/65765
