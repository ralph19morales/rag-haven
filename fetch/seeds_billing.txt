# =====================================================================
# UNPAID BILL OF A DECEASED PATIENT (added 2026-07-29)
# =====================================================================
# Death changes who the creditor may pursue and how. The hospital can no
# longer sue the patient, and it cannot hold the body: RA 9439 (already
# indexed) requires release of the remains, the death certificate and the
# interment documents of a deceased patient over an unpaid bill. What is
# left is a money claim, and it runs against the ESTATE, not the relatives.
#
# Already indexed and load-bearing here — the Civil Code (RA 386) supplies:
#   * Art. 2167  — a person treated while unable to consent (the emergency /
#                  unconscious admission) is liable for the physician's
#                  services; that obligation survives into the estate
#   * Art. 2164-2166 — reimbursement from relatives obliged to give support
#   * Art. 2244(1)-(3) — funeral expenses and expenses of the last illness
#                  are PREFERRED credits against the estate
#   * Arts. 774, 776, 1311 — succession; heirs take the debts with the
#                  inheritance but are not personally liable beyond its value

# The procedure the claim must follow. Rule 86 is the on-point rule: claims
# for "expenses for the last sickness of the decedent" must be filed in the
# estate proceeding within the notice period (6-12 months) or are BARRED
# FOREVER — the statute of non-claims. Rule 88 governs how allowed debts get
# paid out of the estate.
#
# !! AFTER FETCHING THIS ONE, RUN:  python fetch/split_rules_of_court.py
# LawPhil serves Rules 72-109 as a single 148k-character page. Indexed whole it
# made 199 chunks sharing 25 section labels — every rule restarts its own
# numbering, so "Section 2." meant 27 different things — and carried no citable
# law. Rule 86 Sec. 2's deadline retrieved as a bare "Section 4." from a
# law=None document, and the answer denied any deadline existed. The splitter
# writes one file per rule and deletes the combined page; re-fetching restores
# it, so re-run the splitter after any re-fetch.
Rules of Court Part II Special Proceedings (Rules 72 to 109) - Claims Against Estate (Rule 86), Payment of Debts of the Estate (Rule 88) | https://lawphil.net/courts/rules/rc_72-109_proceedings.html
