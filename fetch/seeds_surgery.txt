# Philippine Supreme Court jurisprudence on SURGICAL practice — the
# pre-operative, intra-operative, and post-operative duties of surgeons,
# anesthesiologists, and hospitals.
#
# Format:  <name> | <url>
# Fetch with:
#   python fetch/fetch_seeds.py --file fetch/seeds_surgery.txt --subdir jurisprudence
# (Same subdir as seeds_jurisprudence.txt on purpose — these are the same kind
# of document and the loader/chunker treats them identically.)
#
# Why a separate seed file: seeds_jurisprudence.txt is already fetched, and the
# fetcher skips entries it has ("[have]"). Keeping new work in its own file
# makes a run fast and its output easy to read.
#
# --- IMPORTANT: later rulings on cases already in the corpus ---
# The corpus holds only the ORIGINAL decisions in Ramos and PSI v. Agana, but
# both were substantially modified on reconsideration. Without the resolutions
# below, the RAG can state a superseded holding as current law. Note that the
# chunker tags by G.R. number, so a resolution and its parent decision carry the
# SAME citation label — the year in the entry name is what distinguishes them.

# --- Post-operative / intra-operative: doctrine as finally settled ---
Ramos v. Court of Appeals (G.R. 124354) 2002 Resolution - Anesthesiologist Pre-Operative Evaluation and Surgeon Responsibility on Reconsideration | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/50729
Professional Services Inc. v. Agana (G.R. 126297) 2008 Resolution - Hospital Liability on Reconsideration | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/44890
Professional Services Inc. v. Agana (G.R. 126297) 2010 Resolution - Final Ruling on Corporate Negligence and Ostensible Agency | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/53546

# --- Pre-operative duties: scheduling, attendance, preparation ---

# --- Intra-operative: delegation, supervision, obstetric surgery ---
Dr. Idol Bondoc v. Mantala (G.R. 203080) - Delegation of Delivery to Midwife, Ruptured Uterus, Grave Misconduct of a Government Physician | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/58105

# --- Consent and authority to operate / retrieve organs ---
Dr. Filoteo Alano v. Magud-Logmao (G.R. 175540) - Authority and Consent for Organ Retrieval from a Brain-Dead Patient | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/56800

# --- Pre-operative preparation: nursing duties before a scheduled operation ---
Reyno and De Vera v. Baltazar (G.R. 227775) - Nurses Failure to Perform Blood Sugar Tests and Administer Insulin Before Scheduled Debridement of a Diabetic Patient | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/68785

# --- Hospital duties on admission and TRANSFER of patients ---
