# --- HMO / health-insurer refusal to pay the hospital bill ---
# The recurring fact pattern: the patient is billed because the health plan
# denied the claim. These three set the interpretive rule (a health care
# agreement is akin to non-life insurance, construed strictly against the
# provider) and put the burden of proving an exclusion on the payor.
Blue Cross Health Care v. Olivares (G.R. 169737) - HMO Refusal to Pay, Burden of Proving a Pre-Existing Condition | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/44810
Fortune Medicare v. Amorin (G.R. 195872) - Reimbursement of Hospital Expenses, Ambiguity Construed Against the HMO | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/56685
Philamcare Health Systems v. Court of Appeals (G.R. 125678) - Health Care Agreement as Non-Life Insurance, Concealment and Denial of Claims | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/50579

# --- PhilHealth reimbursement of provider claims ---
Philippine Health Insurance Corp. v. Chinese General Hospital (G.R. 163123) - Denied Provider Claims, Filing Periods, Public Interest Over Technicality | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/43021

# --- Who absorbs the mandatory senior-citizen / PWD discount ---
# These decide whether the 20% discount is a tax credit or merely a tax
# deduction, i.e. how much of the discounted bill the establishment eats.
Carlos Superdrug Corp. v. DSWD (G.R. 166494) - Constitutionality of the 20% Senior Citizen Discount as a Tax Deduction | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/43888
Manila Memorial Park v. Secretary of DSWD (G.R. 175356) - Senior Citizen Discount as Police Power, Not Taking | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/56381
Southern Luzon Drug Corp. v. DSWD (G.R. 199669) - Senior Citizen and PWD Discounts, Tax Deduction Scheme Upheld | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/63029
Drugstores Association of the Philippines v. National Council on Disability Affairs (G.R. 194561) - 20% PWD Discount on Medicines Upheld | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/62361

# --- What an unpaid bill actually costs once it is sued upon ---
# The controlling rule on legal interest (6% per annum, BSP-MB Circular 799),
# which governs any money judgment for an unpaid hospital or professional fee.
Nacar v. Gallery Frames (G.R. 189871) - Legal Interest on Money Judgments, 6% Per Annum Rule | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/56128

# --- The bill of a patient who died: whom can the hospital still pursue? ---
# The rule these settle: the debt is NOT extinguished by death, but the
# creditor loses its ordinary collection suit. It must file a money claim in
# the estate proceeding (Rule 86), and the heirs answer only out of the
# inheritance — not from their own pockets.
Alipio v. Court of Appeals (G.R. 134100) - Creditor Cannot Sue the Surviving Spouse, Claim Must Be Filed Against the Estate | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/36251
Union Bank v. Santibanez (G.R. 149926) - Filing a Money Claim Against the Estate Is Mandatory, Limits of Heirs' Liability | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/43301
Boston Equity Resources v. Court of Appeals (G.R. 173946) - Suing a Deceased Defendant, Estate as Party, Solidary Obligations | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/55921
Stronghold Insurance v. Republic-Asahi Glass (G.R. 147561) - Death Does Not Extinguish a Money Obligation, It Passes to the Estate | https://lawphil.net/judjuris/juri2006/jun2006/gr_147561_2006.html
